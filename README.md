# 📊 Job Market Analysis (Power BI)

## Overview
An end-to-end Power BI dashboard analyzing the Data Science job market — job postings,
salaries, in-demand skills, and hiring trends across industries and states. Built as a
Capstone Project during my Data Analytics Internship at DataMites Institute.

## Business Problem
Job seekers and employers lack a single view connecting job demand, salary benchmarks,
and required skills — making job search and hiring strategy inefficient and reactive.

## Dashboard Pages
1. **Job Market Overview** — top industries, top hiring companies, top job titles,
   and jobs by state
2. **Salary & Education Intelligence** — average salary by state, salary by job title,
   salary vs. education level, company rating vs. salary
3. **Skills & Job Intelligence** — most in-demand skills mapped by job title and
   seniority level

## Key Insights
- Job demand is heavily concentrated in a handful of states, industries, and companies
- Required skills differ sharply by role — targeted upskilling beats generic tool-learning
- Remote hiring can unlock access to lower-competition talent pools

## Tools & Techniques
Power BI · DAX · Data Modeling · Data Cleaning · Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)
